Video Publisher

A private publishing tool that uploads short educational videos to the operator's own YouTube channel.

Video Publisher is not a public product. It is a single-user utility operated by Heck Holdings to schedule and upload its own short-form videos. It has no sign-up, no user accounts, and no third-party users.

What it does

With the channel owner's explicit authorisation, it uploads video files the owner has produced, sets their titles and descriptions, and records the resulting video IDs so a file is never published twice.

What it accesses

Two YouTube Data API scopes, both granted by the channel owner through Google's own consent screen:

youtube.upload — to upload videos to the authorised channel.
youtube.readonly — to confirm which channel the authorisation belongs to before uploading.

It requests nothing else. It does not read viewers' data, comments, or analytics beyond the channel's own public metadata.
